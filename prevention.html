What You Can Do?

Protecting the ocean starts with small daily choices. Here are some simple ways you can help.

At home

  • Reduce Plastic Use
  • Stop Chemical Runoff
  • Dispose of Waste Properly

At the beach

  • Pack out all your trash
  • Avoid single-use plastics
  • Clean up pet waste

Shopping

  • Choose Reusable and Plastic-Free Items
  • Select Ocean-Safe Clothing and Personal Care
  • Buy Sustainable Food
